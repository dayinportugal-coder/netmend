X hook formulas

A hook is the first line that has to work in a busy feed. The useful ones are structures, not topics: a way of starting, which you then fill with a fact you can stand behind. A copied “unpopular opinion” about a subject you do not work in is not a hook. It is a costume.

These are the same families of structure we use when we write for X, and the ones the free X Post Writer rotates through. They are not a guarantee of replies or reach. They are a way of stopping the first line from being a throat-clear or a slogan.

Write the first line last if you need to. Many drafts get better when you delete the opening and start at the first concrete sentence.

Seven structures, with examples

1 · Question + numbered list · example
Structure: a real question, then 3–5 concrete answers.

Example: “If you are hiring a freelancer this month, put these four lines in the brief before you ask for a price:
1. What already exists.
2. What ‘done’ looks like, in files.
3. The drop-dead date, and why.
4. Who signs off — one name.”
2 · Concrete-fact hook · example
Structure: open with a number, a rule, or a dated event that is in your material, then say why it matters.

Example: “We turned off CSV export of raw webhook logs today. It was used by 11 accounts, and two of them were exporting 4 million rows at a time.”
3 · Short twist · example
Structure: one line that goes against the expected slogan, then the correction, then a rule you can use.

Example: “A coffee subscription should be boring. Same bag, same weight, roasted the week it ships.”
4 · Lessons from work · example
Structure: only if you did the work. “I did N. Here is what I would repeat.”

Example: “Three projects that went wrong last year had the same first week: a kickoff call, no written brief, and ‘we will know it when we see it’ on taste.”
5 · Anatomy / parts of a thing · example
Structure: the N parts of a job, named, not padded.

Example: “A complete freelance brief is four parts: existing files, definition of done, a real date, and one person who can say yes.”
6 · Very short hook · example
Structure: the first line does almost all the work. A second line, if you need it, only specifies.

Example: “If your goal has no weekly behaviour, it is a mood.”
7 · Process, in order · example
Structure: the steps as you actually do them, with the stop rule.

Example: “For year-end we ask for the pack eight weeks before the filing date. If it arrives late, we will tell you, early, whether the date is still realistic.”
A first line to avoid · example
“I can not believe more people are not talking about this.”
“A thread 🧵”
“Unpopular opinion:” plus a popular opinion.
“Here’s how I 10x’d…” with no baseline.
“Just listed!” with no constraint.
“Excited to announce…” with no fact.

If you delete the first line and the post gets clearer, delete it.

These are original example drafts, written for this page, for a fictional professional. They are not real posts from a real account, and they are not a promise of reach, clients or sales. Adapt the structure; replace every fact with one you can stand behind.

How to use a formula without sounding like a formula

  • Fill it with your own material. The structure is reusable. The facts are not. If you do not have a number, do not invent one; use a rule, a sequence, or a distinction instead.
  • Keep the first line specific. Names, objects, dates, constraints. “People make this mistake with onboarding” is weaker than “People were pasting an API token into the workspace ID field.”
  • Do not stack formulas. One hook, then the body. A question and a twist and a list of lessons is a mess.
  • Match the hook to the length. A very short hook can be the whole post. A question + list needs the list in the same post, or immediately in a thread.

FAQ

Do hooks still matter if I have few followers?

Yes. Especially then. People who do not know you only have the first line and the first screen.

Can I reuse the same structure every day?

You can rotate a handful. Using the same one every time makes a feed feel automated, even when it is not.

What if I do not have a striking number?

Use a process, a distinction, a short twist, or a very short claim you can defend. A fake number is worse than a quiet true sentence.
