      How to build the SEPA file

      One payment per row. The beneficiary name, the IBAN and the amount are required. The reference and the BIC are optional.

      Step 1

      Download the template or use your own sheet. Headers the page recognises: Name, IBAN, Amount, Reference, BIC.

      Step 2

      Fill in the debtor. The name, IBAN, BIC, date and batch-booking choice stay on this device only.

      Step 3

      Read Problems and the control sum. Download the XML and import it in online banking. It is not sent from here.

      What Problems checks

      An error blocks the XML. A warning still lets you download, but read it. Accents are converted to the SEPA character set (á becomes a, ç becomes c, ñ becomes n) and the change is listed.

      IBAN and NIB

      The IBAN has to pass mod 97 and match the country length. On a Portuguese IBAN, the 21-digit NIB also has to pass its check digit.

      Amount

      Greater than zero, at most two decimal places, up to 999.999.999,99 €. The control sum is the sum of the rows, in cents.

      Name and reference

      The name fits in 70 characters and the reference in 140, after conversion. A repeated EndToEndId is replaced with a unique id.

      BIC optional

      Leave it empty and the debtor bank is written as NOTPROVIDED, which the Banco de Portugal C2B manual allows. If you fill it in, it has to be 8 or 11 characters.

      Where to import it

      The menu name is not the same at every bank. Look for an import of transfer files, batch payments or SEPA files, as XML.

      Caixa Geral de Depósitos, on its Canal Multibancário page, lists “Transferências a Crédito e Transferências Múltiplas C2B XML pain.001.001.03” (cgd.pt, checked 21 November 2026). For other banks, use the name on your own screen.

      The character set and NOTPROVIDED follow the Banco de Portugal Customer-to-Bank XML manual (C2B), section 3.3 and message pain.001.001.03. Accent conversion follows the EPC Latin table (á→a and the other letters in that table).

      Questions

      Does the file leave my computer?

      No. Reading, the checks and the XML all happen in this browser. There is no account and no upload. Closing the tab does not forget a debtor saved on this device: use Clear data on this browser for that.

      What is this SEPA file?

      It is a credit-transfer XML, ISO 20022 pain.001.001.03. It pays suppliers or salaries in a batch, in euro, to IBANs in the SEPA area. pain.001.001.09 is also available. Check with your bank which one it imports.

      How does the bank import the file?

      In online banking, look for an import of transfer files or batch payments. Caixa Geral de Depósitos, on its Canal Multibancário page, lists “Transferências a Crédito e Transferências Múltiplas C2B XML pain.001.001.03” (cgd.pt, checked 21 November 2026). Other banks use a different menu name.

      What is checked before download?

      The IBAN (mod-97 and the country length), the Portuguese NIB check on PT accounts, the BIC format when one is present, the amount (greater than zero, at most two decimal places), the SEPA character set, and the length of the name (70) and the reference (140). Accents are converted (á becomes a) and the change is listed under Problems. A repeated EndToEndId is made unique. The same IBAN with the same amount raises a warning.

      How many payments fit in the file?

      The free version includes 50 payments per file. Pro coming soon. The button does not take a payment and shows no price.

      Does this replace the bank?

      No. The page only builds the file. It does not send payments. Check the IBANs, the amounts and the date at your bank before you authorise the debit.
