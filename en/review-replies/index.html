FAQ

Is this AI? Where do my reviews go?

The free version builds the drafts in your browser from reply structures we wrote and tested, and picks up what the review mentions (food, staff, waiting time, price, cleanliness and so on). Nothing is sent to Netmend or to any AI service. There is no server for this tool.

Should I really reply to every review?

Replying to good and bad reviews shows future customers that someone is listening. Keep it short, thank the person, mention one specific thing, and for complaints offer a way to continue the conversation privately.

What counts as one use?

One click of “Write 3 replies”. The free version allows 3 per day in this browser. The counter is stored in localStorage, so it is per browser.

Will it post the reply for me?

No. You copy the draft you like and paste it in Google Business Profile (Reviews → Reply) yourself.

Which languages?

The free version writes in English. Portuguese, Spanish and French are planned for Pro.
