Create the Google Form in 1 minute

  1. Open script.google.com → New project (signed in to your Google account).
  2. Replace the code with the script below (it is already copied to your clipboard) and click Run. Approve the Forms permission (it is your own script).
  3. Open Execution log: it shows the edit link and the link to share with students. Answers are graded automatically.
