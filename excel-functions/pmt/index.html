What PMT does

Mortgage and loan calculators. Names: PGTO, PAGO, VPM, RMZ, RATA. The rate and the period must be in the same unit, and European decimals matter.

Returns the payment for a loan based on a constant interest rate and a constant payment schedule.

Syntax and a worked example in 6 languages

English Excel uses commas between arguments and a dot decimal. Most European Excel versions use semicolons and a decimal comma. TRUE / FALSE also change name. The translator below applies all three.

English — PMT
=PMT(rate, nper, pv, [fv], [type])
=PMT(0.05/12,360,200000)

=PGTO(0,05/12;360;200000)

Common mistakes

  • If the annual rate is 5% and payments are monthly, use 5%/12 (or 0,05/12 in European Excel), not 5%.
  • nper is the number of payments (30 years monthly = 360), not the number of years.
  • PMT returns a negative number if the present value is a positive loan amount (cash-flow sign convention). Wrap it in ABS or enter the loan as negative, consistently.

FAQ

What is PMT in Portuguese?

PGTO. Spanish PAGO, French VPM, German RMZ, Italian RATA.

Why is the result negative?

Excel treats the loan (pv) as cash you received and the payment as cash you pay out. Use ABS, or enter pv as negative, and stay consistent.

European decimal?

In Portuguese or German Excel write 0,05/12, not 0.05/12.
