FAQ

What is VLOOKUP in Portuguese?

PROCV (Portugal and Brazil). In Spanish BUSCARV, French RECHERCHEV, German SVERWEIS, Italian CERCA.VERT.

Why does my formula show an error after I paste it?

Usually the separator: English Excel uses commas between arguments, most European Excel versions use semicolons, and a decimal comma. The translator fixes both.

Can I change Excel to English function names?

Only by changing Excel's display/editing language in Office settings. Many people prefer to keep their language and translate the formula instead.
