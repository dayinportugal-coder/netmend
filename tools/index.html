How the free AI works

Google gives a free Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com. Paste it once in any Netmend AI tool; it is saved only in your browser and shared by all Netmend tools on that device. Your text and files go from your browser straight to Google, never to Netmend. The finance tools on this page do not use that key: the statement converter and e-fatura run locally.
