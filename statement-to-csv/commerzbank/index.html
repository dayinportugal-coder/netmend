The Commerzbank statement format

DatesGerman statements are day-first (DD.MM.YYYY). A US-locale spreadsheet will swap days below 13. Set the date column to TT.MM.JJJJ / DD.MM.YYYY or to text before you import.
NumbersEuro, decimal comma (1.234,56). Use a semicolon-separated CSV for German Excel. If 1,25 becomes 125, the decimal setting is wrong.

Where to download the official file

Two official files, from Commerzbank’s service pages.

Kontoauszüge (official statements) — “Kontoauszüge im Postfach finden”:

  • In the Banking App: after sign-in, open Profil in the navigation bar, then Postfach. Documents are listed by date. Open the monthly statement to see the movements.
  • In Online Banking: open the electronic Postfach, filter by the account or card you need, and open the matching Kontoauszug.

Commerzbank notes that the Postfach is already unlocked for most functions; fully activating it switches existing accounts, depots and cards to electronic delivery. After a product is closed, later statements are under “ehemalige Produkte”. Year-end or account-closure statements are also sent to the address on file. In the last week of the year no weekly statement is produced; there is a year-end statement instead, and the first statement of the new year does not contain the previous year.

Umsatz export (transaction list) — “Wie kann ich meine Umsätze exportieren?”. In Online Banking, open the Umsatzübersicht, select the account, click PDF / CSV above the transactions, then either:

  • Umsätze exportieren — choose which transactions, then PDF or CSV, then Exportieren; or
  • Umsätze auswählen — tick individual rows, then Exportieren, then PDF or CSV.

What the PDF contains

The Kontoauszug in the Postfach is the official statement for that period. It is the file to keep for tax, landlords and auditors. An Umsatz-PDF or CSV exported from the transaction list is a list of movements you selected; it is not automatically a substitute for the official Kontoauszug, and it may not carry the same printed opening and closing balance or letterhead.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

For recent activity, prefer Commerzbank’s official CSV from the Umsatzübersicht (PDF / CSV → Umsätze exportieren). Commerzbank does not publish the column headings on that service page — open the first row of your own export. German Excel expects a semicolon separator and a decimal comma.

Convert the official Kontoauszug PDF when you need the rows to tick against that statement, when you were only sent the PDF, or when the period is no longer on the live Umsatzübersicht. After conversion, check the printed Saldo against the sum of the rows.

What to watch for

  • Need a spreadsheet of recent bookings? Use the official Umsatz CSV and skip the PDF.
  • Need the official monthly statement? Download the Kontoauszug from the Postfach. Convert it only if you also need columns.
  • Closed-product statements sit under “ehemalige Produkte” — they are easy to miss if you only filter the live account.
  • Do not merge a card statement and a current-account Kontoauszug in one file without naming the source.

FAQ

Does Commerzbank already give me a CSV?

Yes, of Umsätze (the transaction list): in the Umsatzübersicht click PDF / CSV and export as CSV. The official Kontoauszug in the Postfach is a PDF.

Is an Umsatz CSV the same as a Kontoauszug?

No. The Kontoauszug is the official statement in the Postfach. The Umsatz export is a list of transactions you chose to download.

Where do statements for a closed account go?

Commerzbank says that after the last statement of a deleted product, you will find the statements in the Postfach under “ehemalige Produkte”.
