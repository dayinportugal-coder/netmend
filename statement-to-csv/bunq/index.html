Where to download the official file

Official path, from bunq Help (“How do I export a bank statement?”). Your account must be verified or bunq will not export a statement.

From Profile: tap Profile (top left) → Accounting → Export Statement. Choose the bank account, the period and the format (PDF, CSV or MT940), then Export Statement.

From a specific account: Home → the account → Settings → Export statement → period and format.

On the web: sign in at web.bunq.com, open the account, Settings → Export statement, choose the date and format, then export.

Closed accounts: Home → Profile → Archive → Bank Accounts → the account → Export Statement.

Other official documents, from “What types of bank statements can I export?”: a card statement (Profile → Accounting → Card Statements, or from the card’s settings); a single-transaction statement (open the payment → Export); an annual overview (available each January for the previous year, under Accounting → Annual Overview, as PDF); and a statement of fees for the previous year, listed there too. A signed and stamped statement is not the self-export — message bunq Support for that. Self-exports carry no signature or stamp. Image attachments can be included on a PDF export only (toggle “Include Attachments”).

What the PDF contains

A bunq bank-statement PDF is generated from your payment history for one IBAN and period. It shows your name and IBAN (bunq notes it can be used as proof of ownership). bunq cannot filter the official file to incoming-only, or remove individual payments, or change the layout — those limits also apply to signed copies from Support. A card statement is a different document (name, last four digits, the name on the card, and the IBAN used for each payment).

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

If you can still generate the official CSV, use it and skip the PDF. bunq does not publish the CSV column names on those Help articles — open the first row of your own export. Prefer that official CSV for accounting imports; use MT940 if your accountant asked for it.

Convert the PDF when someone sent you only the PDF, when you need to tick rows against a signed paper copy, or when you want a spreadsheet from a card statement that you only have as a PDF. After conversion, check the totals against the PDF before you import the file.

What to watch for

  • Verify the account first. Unverified accounts cannot export a statement.
  • Need a stamp or signature? Do not convert a self-export and hope — ask bunq Support for the signed document, then convert that PDF if you also need columns.
  • Annual overviews and fee statements are year-end documents, not a substitute for a period statement.
  • Keep one IBAN per file. bunq statements are per bank account.

FAQ

Does bunq already give me a CSV?

Yes. Export Statement lets you choose PDF, CSV or MT940, in the app and on web.bunq.com, once the account is verified.

Can I get a signed statement myself?

No. Self-exports have no signature or stamp. bunq Help says to message Support for a signed and stamped statement, and that they will not change its layout or filter out payments.

What language is the statement in?

Help says documents other than the French RIB are currently available in English only.
