Where to download the official file

Official path, from Millennium bcp Help (“Posso fazer download do meu extrato de conta?”, checked 11/10/2026 and still current on 12/11/2026):
  1. Log in on millenniumbcp.pt and open Contas.
  2. Go to Documentos em formato digital → Consultar extratos e notas de lançamento.
  3. Tick the box next to the extracto and click Download.

The bank’s Extrato Digital page says the last 12 months stay available on the site and in the app. In the app the path is typically Contas → detail of the account → Extratos. You can also adhere to “Envio por email” so a PDF arrives each month.

What the PDF contains

Portuguese current-account extractos list each movement with a movement date, often a value date, a descrição (SEPA references and extra lines often wrap under the same movement), a débito column, a crédito column, and a running saldo. Debits and credits are separate columns — they are not a single signed amount. Multi-line descriptions and page breaks are the usual reason a naive copy-paste splits one movement into two rows.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The bank’s official download is the PDF extracto, not a spreadsheet. After conversion you want a row per movement: Data, Data valor (if present), Descrição, Débito, Crédito, Saldo. Our tool outputs Date, Description, Amount (debits negative, credits positive) and Balance. If a column lands in the wrong place, try “Every text row” and we can tidy the layout for you.

What to watch for

  • Set this tool to European numbers and “Excel in Europe” before you convert.
  • After the download, check that opening saldo + créditos − débitos equals the closing saldo on the PDF. That single check catches almost every misread row.
  • If the PDF is password-protected, open it and “print to PDF” first so the tool can read the text.

FAQ

Does Millennium bcp offer a CSV?

The official document in Documentos em formato digital is a PDF. This converter is for that file. Some screens show a movement list you can export — if you have a CSV already, use that.

Why does Excel show all the amounts in one column?

Excel in Portugal expects a semicolon CSV. Choose “Excel in Europe” in the tool, or open the file with Data → From Text/CSV.

What about data valor vs data do movimento?

They can differ. For bookkeeping, most people keep both and book on the movement date unless their accountant asks for the value date.
