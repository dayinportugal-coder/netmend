Where to download the official file

Official PDF statements, from PNC “Online Statements and Documents”:

In Online Banking: sign on, select Accounts, open the account, select Account Quick View, then Statements and Documents, then View PDF.

In the PNC Mobile app: sign in, open the More menu, then Statements and Documents. Choose Online statements, Account documents or Other documents, select the account, then the year.

How far back, from PNC’s legal notes on that page: up to 7 years for checking and savings, auto loans and unsecured instalment loans; up to 4 years for credit cards; up to 5 years for mortgage and home equity; up to 10 years for investment accounts. Online history may not be available for closed accounts. Paperless enrolment is per account (Profile and Settings → Alerts and Communication → Paperless in Online Banking, or Paperless Settings in the app).

Quicken and QuickBooks: PNC documents a live bank feed, and also a manual Web Connect export. In PNC Online Banking you can export transactions and choose .QFX for Quicken or .QBO for QuickBooks. PNC does not publish a public official CSV guide on those pages — if you need a spreadsheet, convert the official PDF, or open the QFX/QBO in the software it was made for.

What the PDF contains

The official statement is the PDF under Statements and Documents. Checking, savings, card and loan statements have different layouts and retention periods — do not mix a card PDF with a checking conversion. The PDF is also the file PNC points former FirstBank customers to when transactions around a conversion are missing from Quicken or QuickBooks.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

PNC’s officially documented download formats for software are .QFX (Quicken) and .QBO (QuickBooks), not a public CSV specification. If you need columns in Excel, convert the PDF and keep date, description, amount or debit/credit, and the running balance where it is printed. Tick the opening and closing balances before you use the file.

If you already use QuickBooks or Quicken, the official QBO or QFX export (or the bank feed) is the cleaner path — you should not need a PDF conversion for recent activity.

What to watch for

  • For QuickBooks, use PNC’s official .QBO export or the bank feed. For Quicken, use .QFX.
  • For a plain spreadsheet, or for months that only exist as PDFs, convert the statement and check the printed totals.
  • Card statements are kept for fewer years (up to 4) than checking (up to 7). Do not assume a 2019 card statement is still online.

FAQ

Does PNC give me a CSV?

Not in any public official guide we could cite. PNC does officially let you export .QFX for Quicken and .QBO for QuickBooks from Online Banking. For a spreadsheet, convert the official PDF.

How far back are PNC statements?

Up to 7 years for checking and savings, 4 years for credit cards, 5 years for mortgage and home equity, 10 years for investment accounts, according to PNC’s notes on the Online Statements page.

Where do I find the PDF in the app?

More menu → Statements and Documents, then the document type, the account and the year.
