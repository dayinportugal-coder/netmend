The ABN AMRO statement format

DatesDutch statements are day-first (DD-MM-YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY). A US-locale spreadsheet will swap days below 13. Official monthly statements are stored in the language (Dutch or English) that was set as your preference at the end of that month — you cannot regenerate an old statement in the other language.
NumbersEuro, European decimal comma on Dutch documents (1.234,56). Official XLS downloads should already be a spreadsheet; still check that 1,25 is a quarter and not one hundred and twenty-five.

Where to download the official file

Official path, from ABN AMRO “Rekeningafschriften en bij- en afschrijvingen downloaden”.

Monthly statements (rekeningafschriften) — PDF only, with your address, available up to 20 years back (the app text says from 2005). In Internet Bankieren, choose the account holder, the account and the type of overview, tick the statement, and download the PDF. You can download up to 50 statements at once, delivered as a ZIP. In the app: Profile → Rekeningen en betalen → Afschriften en jaaroverzicht, then the holder, the type of overview, the account if needed, the period, tick and download. A new monthly statement is made on the last working day of the month and is available on the second working day of the next month, in the language then set in Internet Bankieren.

Transactions (bij- en afschrijvingen) — last 18 months, with your address, in PDF, TXT, MT940, XLS or CAMT.053. In Internet Bankieren or the app, choose the holder, the format, a start and end date, and download from the icon next to the account. For English transaction overviews, switch to the English Internet Bankieren environment before you download (you choose the language at download time for this file, unlike stored monthly statements).

Weekend trap: if the end date you pick falls on a weekend, Saturday and Sunday transactions are not included. ABN AMRO tells you to use the next working day as the end date. The same applies when a month-end falls on a weekend for a one-month download — those days appear on the next month’s statement.

What the PDF contains

The rekeningafschrift PDF is the official monthly document. You cannot search it for one counterparty the way you can search the live transaction list, and you cannot download a filtered “only this supermarket” statement — ABN AMRO says you can filter on screen but not download that filter. Older than 18 months, this PDF is the only official download.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

For the last 18 months, prefer ABN AMRO’s official XLS (or CAMT.053 / MT940 if your accountant asked for those). That is already a structured file; you should not need to convert a PDF for recent activity. ABN AMRO does not list the XLS column headings on that help page — open the first row of your own download.

Convert a rekeningafschrift PDF when the period is older than 18 months, when you were only sent the PDF, or when you need the rows to tick against that official monthly statement. After conversion, use a European number format and check the printed opening and closing balances.

What to watch for

  • Need recent transactions in Excel? Download bij- en afschrijvingen as XLS, and end the date range on a working day.
  • Need 2019 for a tax enquiry? Download the monthly PDF statements (up to 50 at a time in a ZIP) and convert those.
  • Do not expect to change the language of a statement that was already stored — only new monthly statements follow the language now set.
  • A joint-account change or a name change can hide older statements of the previous account. ABN AMRO says to contact them for those.

FAQ

Does ABN AMRO already give me Excel?

Yes, for the last 18 months: download bij- en afschrijvingen and choose XLS (or MT940 or CAMT.053). Official monthly statements going further back are PDF only.

Why are weekend payments missing?

If your end date is a Saturday or Sunday, ABN AMRO does not include those weekend transactions. Use the next working day as the end date.

Can I download many statements at once?

Yes — up to 50 rekeningafschriften in one ZIP file.
