The Chase statement format

DatesUS format MM/DD/YYYY in both the activity CSV and the PDF (for example 11/12/2026 is 12 November, not 11 December). A day-first spreadsheet will swap any day below 13.
NumbersUS format: 1,234.56 (comma thousands, dot decimal). Amounts in the official CSV are signed: withdrawals and purchases are negative, deposits and payments are positive.

Where to download the official file

Activity CSV (the file you want for Excel):

  1. Sign in at chase.com and open the account.
  2. In Account Activity, use the download icon (or, in some views, Manage spending / Spending Planner → Download Transactions).
  3. Choose Spreadsheet (CSV) or Excel, pick the account and date range, and download.

The exact label moves around as Chase updates the site; look for Download next to the activity list. Searching the account first can be required before a longer date range appears.

Official PDF statement: Main Menu → Statements & documents → select the account and year → save the statement PDF.

What the PDF contains

A Chase checking or savings PDF is the official monthly statement (beginning balance, deposits, withdrawals, ending balance, cheque numbers). Transaction dates are MM/D or MM/DD/YYYY depending on the line. Credit-card PDFs add a post date, a category and a payment-due section — do not mix them with checking in one file.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Checking/savings activity CSV typically has: Details, Posting Date, Description, Amount, Type, Balance, Check or Slip # Credit-card CSV typically has: Transaction Date, Post Date, Description, Category, Type, Amount and sometimes Memo. Amount is signed. Prefer this official CSV. Use our PDF converter when the activity download will not cover the months you need, or when a client only sent the PDF.

What to watch for

  • Never assume a “Chase” file is UK-style. Chase UK is a different bank.
  • Import dates as MM/DD/YYYY or as text, then check a date after the 12th.
  • If QBO’s bank feed misses authorised users on a card, the official activity CSV (or the PDF) is the fallback — see our note in the FAQ.

FAQ

Does Chase already give me a CSV?

Yes, from Account Activity (Spreadsheet / CSV) for a date range the site still offers. Official monthly statements are PDFs.

Why are my dates in the wrong month?

Chase uses MM/DD/YYYY. 03/09/2026 is 9 March, not 3 September. Import as US dates, or look at a day after the 12th.

Checking and credit card in one file?

No. The headers are different (Posting Date and Balance vs Transaction Date and Post Date). Convert or download them separately.
