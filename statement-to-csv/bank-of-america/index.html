Where to download the official file

Activity CSV: sign in, open the account, choose a date range in activity, and download a CSV or Excel file (often up to 18 months, depending on the product). Do this for each account.

Official PDF: Statements & Documents (or the Statements tab) → pick the account and period → download the monthly statement.

What the PDF contains

A Bank of America checking PDF is the official monthly statement: beginning balance, deposits, withdrawals, other credits or debits, and the ending balance, with transaction dates in US format. Credit-card statements are a different layout (transaction date, post date, payment due). Convert them separately.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

A typical checking/savings activity CSV has the columns: Date, Description, Amount, Running Bal.

Important: the file often starts with a balance-summary block (account name, totals, a few blank rows) before the real header. If you import the file as-is, those lines become “transactions”. Delete everything above the Date, Description, Amount, Running Bal. row, then import. Amount is signed.

What to watch for

  • Open the CSV in a text editor first and find the real header row before you let Excel guess.
  • Import dates as MM/DD/YYYY. Check a transaction you remember on a day after the 12th.
  • Use our PDF converter when the activity download does not reach the months you need.

Is Amount signed?

Yes in the usual checking/savings export: withdrawals are negative.
