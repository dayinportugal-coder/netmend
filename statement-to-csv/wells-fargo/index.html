Where to download the official file

Activity CSV: sign in, open the account, go to activity for a date range, and choose Download Account Activity (CSV). Do each account separately.

Official PDF: Statements & Documents → select the account and period → download the statement PDF, especially when you need months the activity download no longer covers.

What the PDF contains

A typical Wells Fargo checking PDF lists Date (often M/D, no year), Check Number, Description, Deposits/Credits, Withdrawals/Debits and an ending daily balance. Because the year may be missing on each line, a January statement that includes late December is the dangerous case. Credit-card statements differ — keep them in their own file.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The Download Account Activity CSV commonly has no header row. The columns, in order, are typically: Date, Amount, *, a check-number field (often blank), Description

There may be no running balance. Because there is no header, the first transaction is not a title row. Import with “no headers”, then name the columns yourself. Amount is signed.

What to watch for

  • Do not tick “My data has headers” until you have looked at row 1 of the official CSV.
  • When converting a PDF, add the year from the statement period to every date.
  • Use our converter for PDFs; do not run the official CSV through a PDF tool.

FAQ

Why is there no header in my CSV?

That is how Wells Fargo’s activity download is commonly built. Import with no headers and assign column names yourself.

The PDF date has no year. What do I do?

Take the year from the statement period. On a statement that spans December and January, assign the year line by line.

Is this the same as a Wells Fargo credit-card file?

No. Download or convert cards separately; the columns differ.
