Where to download the official file

Official statements and other documents are held in Citi Online under Statements and Documents (Citi’s servicing page for statements and document services). After you sign in, open the account and look for Statements or Statements and Documents, then download the PDF for the period you need.

Citi does not publish a clear, public, step-by-step guide for a US personal-account CSV or OFX export on a help page we could open on 13/11/2026. If you see a download or “download account activity” control once you are signed in, use it when it covers your dates, and treat the formats listed in that dialog as the authority. For anything a lender or accountant will rely on, keep the official PDF as well.

This page is for US Citi Online. Citibank sites in other countries (and Citi commercial / CitiManager card portals) use different menus and file types — do not follow these notes there.

What the PDF contains

A Citi statement PDF is the official monthly document: account details, the period, a summary, and the transactions for that period. Credit-card and deposit-account statements are different products. Pending or authorised-but-not-posted items can appear in the live activity view and not on the statement, or the other way around — always convert the PDF you were given, not a screenshot of the activity feed.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

There is no official public Citi CSV layout for US personal accounts that we could cite. After a PDF conversion, keep date, description, amount or debit/credit, and the running balance where it is printed. Tick the opening and closing balances before you import the file.

If you did get an on-screen activity export, compare the first and last dates and the total of the rows with the official PDF. An activity export is not automatically the same thing as the statement.

What to watch for

  • Use the official PDF for anything a third party will rely on. Convert it for bookkeeping.
  • Do not mix a checking statement and a Citi card statement in one spreadsheet without a column that names the account.
  • Older or archived statements may take time to become available after you request them — if a PDF is missing, wait and try again in Citi Online before assuming it is gone.

FAQ

Does Citibank give me a CSV?

Not in any public official how-to we could cite for US personal accounts. Official statements are PDFs in Statements and Documents. If you see a download control on account activity, use it and keep the first row of that file as your template.

Is this the same as Chase or Citibank in another country?

No. This page is for US Citi Online. Other Citi countries, and Chase (JPMorgan Chase), are different banks with different files.

Can I convert a screenshot of Citi activity?

No. This tool needs a digital PDF with a text layer. A screenshot is an image.
