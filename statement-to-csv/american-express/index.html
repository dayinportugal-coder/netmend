Where to download the official file

Official path, from American Express US Help (“How do I access my transaction history and billing statements?”):

View transaction history and billing statements through your online account. Amex says your previous two years of transactions can be viewed electronically, and that you can sort, filter and download as a PDF, Excel or CSV file.

To access a PDF copy of your statement, including older statements that have been archived, select the statement you need from the list. Archived statements will be available online within 24 hours.

The exact menu names on the site can change; look for activity, statements or billing statements after you sign in. This page is for the US Help article. American Express sites in other countries use different wording and may offer different formats.

What the PDF contains

The billing-statement PDF is the official monthly document. It includes the account summary (previous balance, payments, new charges, the amount due and the payment date), plus the list of transactions, fees and interest for that cycle. A transaction-history download for a custom date range is not the same file: it may not include the statement summary or the official minimum payment. When a lender, tax enquiry or employer asks for “your Amex statements”, send the official PDF.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

For the last two years, prefer Amex’s own Excel or CSV download — that is the official spreadsheet. Amex does not publish the column names in that Help article; open the first row of your own file. Watch the sign of payments versus purchases, and any “extended” or instalment-plan rows that might also appear in the cycle’s new-charges total.

Convert the billing-statement PDF when the period is older than the two-year activity download, when you were only sent the PDF, or when you need the rows to tick against that statement’s totals. After conversion, check payments, new charges and the closing balance against the front page of the PDF.

What to watch for

  • If you use QuickBooks, upload card activity into a credit-card account, not a bank account. Intuit warns that card CSVs may show payments as negative amounts because they reduce the card balance.
  • Membership Rewards or instalment-plan lines can sit next to ordinary purchases. Do not treat every printed line as a cash amount until you have compared the total with the statement summary.
  • If an older PDF is not there immediately, Amex says archived statements become available within 24 hours of being selected.

FAQ

Does American Express already give me Excel or CSV?

Yes, for US accounts. Official help says the previous two years of transactions can be downloaded as PDF, Excel or CSV. Use that when it covers your dates.

When do I still need to convert the PDF?

When the statement is older than that two-year activity window, when it was archived and you only have the PDF, or when you need the rows to match a specific billing statement’s totals.

Is a transaction-history download the same as a statement?

No. The official billing statement is the PDF you pick from the statement list. The two-year download is activity you can sort and filter.
