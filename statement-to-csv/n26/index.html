The N26 statement format

DatesEnglish-language PDFs commonly print dates as DD/MM/YYYY. German-language PDFs may use DD.MM.YYYY. The period is written on the first page (Statement period / Kontoauszug).
NumbersThis is the quirk: although N26 is a German bank, many English (and some PDF) statements use a dot decimal (1,234.56), not German 1.234,56. German-market files can still use a comma decimal. Where to download the official file

Official path, from N26 Support (“How to get a bank statement”):

Monthly balance statements (PDF)

  1. In the app: Finances → choose the account or Space → gear icon → Balance statements → pick the month.
  2. On the web: Home → the account or Space → Downloads → under Bank statements, pick the month. You can also go via Spaces → Settings.

Account activity report (custom dates, PDF or CSV) — on the web only:

  1. Home → the account or Space → Downloads.
  2. Under Account Activity, enter start and end dates, choose PDF or CSV, and download.

N26 says these documents are official and valid without a signature or stamp. They show your name, IBAN and BIC.

What the PDF contains

Monthly PDFs are headed “Bank Statement” (English) or “Kontoauszug” (German). A summary lists Opening Balance / Anfangssaldo and Closing Balance / Schlusssaldo. Transaction lines typically contain:

  • Date
  • Description (merchant or counterparty; German files may attach an N26 category)
  • Amount, often with an explicit sign and the currency (for example -EUR 38.75)
  • Running balance

Transfers to and from Spaces appear in the same list as spending. Treat Space transfers as movements between your own pots, not as income or expenses, if you are building a budget.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Prefer the official Account Activity CSV when you can still log in on the web: it already has rows you can open in Excel. Use our converter for monthly PDFs you were sent, or for months where the CSV is no longer offered.

FAQ

Does N26 already give me a CSV?

Yes, as an Account Activity report on the web, for a date range you choose. Monthly balance statements are PDF only.

Is the number format German or US?

It depends on the file. Many English N26 PDFs use 1,234.56 even though the bank is German. If a total is clearly wrong, switch the number format in the tool and convert again.

Are Space transfers real spending?

They move money between your own N26 Spaces. Flag them so they don’t inflate income or expenses.
