Where to download the official file

Official path, from Revolut Help (“Download an account statement for a specific currency”):
  1. In the Revolut app, go to Home.
  2. Below your balance, tap Accounts and select the currency.
  3. Tap More (…) and select Statement.
  4. Choose the timeframe and a document type (PDF or Excel) and tap Generate.

The same flow exists in the web app at app.revolut.com. Revolut issues one statement per currency. Official statements include the account holder details, local and international BIC/IBAN, a balance summary, and pending, completed and reverted transactions, including Pocket transactions.

What the PDF contains

A Revolut PDF is the official document. Transaction lines typically show a date, a description, an amount and a running balance. Pending and reverted items can appear next to completed ones, which is why a mid-month PDF can list rows that never hit the closing balance.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The “Excel” option is a CSV (it is not a real .xlsx). Current exports use this header: Type,Product,Started Date,Completed Date,Description,Amount,Fee,Currency,State,Balance

  • Amount is signed: money out is negative.
  • Fee is a separate column. An ATM fee is not included in Amount.
  • Started Date is when the payment began; Completed Date is when it settled. A card payment on the 31st can complete in the next month.
  • State may be COMPLETED, pending or reverted. Do not import pending or reverted rows as if they moved the balance.

Revolut does not officially publish this layout and has changed it before. Compare the first row of your own file before you rely on a template.

What to watch for

  • If you can still generate the Excel/CSV, use that and skip the PDF.
  • When importing into Xero or similar, rewrite the timestamp as a plain date (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD) and add each non-zero fee as its own row on the same date.
  • After a PDF conversion, add Amount + Fee and check the running Balance against the PDF.

FAQ

Does Revolut already give me a CSV?

Yes. Choose Excel rather than PDF when you generate the statement. Use our converter only if you already have a PDF and cannot regenerate the file.

Why don’t the totals match?

Pending or reverted rows, or fees sitting in their own column, are the usual cause. Keep only COMPLETED rows and add Amount and Fee before you compare the balance.

Can I put several currencies in one file?

Not usefully. Download one statement per currency and keep them in separate sheets or files.
