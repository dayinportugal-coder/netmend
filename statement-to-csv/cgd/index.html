Where to download the official file

Official documents live in Caixadirecta’s Documentos Digitais area (cgd.pt describes Extrato Conta à Ordem, Extrato Global, Extrato Cartão de Crédito and others). The last 24 months of digital documents stay available to consult, copy or save.
  1. Log in to Caixadirecta (caixadirecta.cgd.pt or the app).
  2. Open Documentos Digitais (or Extratos / Consultas, depending on the profile the site loads).
  3. Download the Extrato Conta à Ordem as a PDF. The Extrato Global is a monthly pack of several products — convert each current account separately if you need a clean sheet.

Some Caixadirecta movement screens also let you export a period as CSV. If you still have that option, use the official CSV and keep the PDF as the proof document.

What the PDF contains

A CGD current-account extracto typically lists Data movimento, Data valor, Descritivo, Débito, Crédito and Saldo contabilístico (book balance). The book balance is the one to reconcile on every row. Where an available balance is also printed, it can include holds or overdrafts and should not be used for the check. Débito and Crédito are separate columns. The Extrato Integrado / Extrato Global can group several products in one PDF — only the current-account section should go into a transaction spreadsheet.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Official digital statements are PDFs. After conversion you want one row per movement, with both dates if present, the full descritivo, money out, money in, and the book balance. Our tool outputs a signed Amount (debits negative) plus Balance. Transfers between your own CGD accounts (for example conta à ordem to poupança) appear as a debit on one statement and a credit on the other — exclude them if you are measuring spending.

What to watch for

  • Reconcile against the saldo contabilístico, not the available balance.
  • Export or convert each account on its own. There is no useful “all my CGD accounts in one CSV” for spending analysis.
  • Set European numbers and a semicolon CSV before you convert.

FAQ

Extrato Global or Extrato Conta à Ordem?

Use the Conta à Ordem statement for a transaction list. The Extrato Global is a monthly pack of several products and is harder to turn into one clean table.

Which saldo should I check?

Saldo contabilístico (book balance). The available balance, when printed, can include holds.

Does Caixadirecta offer CSV?

Some movement screens do. If you can export CSV, use that and keep the PDF as the official document. This tool is for the PDF extracto.
