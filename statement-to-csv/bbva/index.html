Where to download the official file

In BBVA banca online (bbva.es) or the app:
  1. Open Cuentas, select the account and the movement period you need.
  2. For the official extracto, use the account menu and look for Extractos or Correspondencia, then download the PDF.
  3. Some movement screens also let you export a list for a period. If a spreadsheet or CSV is offered, use that for Excel work and keep the PDF as the official document.

What the PDF contains

A typical Spanish current-account extracto lists Fecha de operación, Fecha valor, Concepto or Movimiento, Importe (sometimes as separate cargo/abono columns) and Saldo. Descriptions and transfer references often wrap onto a second line under the same date. Credit-card extractos are a different document — convert them separately.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

After conversion you want one row per movement, with both dates if present, the full concepto, a signed amount (or cargo/abono) and the running saldo. Our tool outputs Date, Description, Amount and Balance. If cargo and abono come out in the wrong sign, try “Every text row” or send us the PDF.

What to watch for

  • Set European numbers and a semicolon CSV before you convert.
  • Reconcile opening saldo ± movements to the closing saldo on the PDF.
  • This page describes the Spanish extracto. BBVA Mexico or Argentina files often use a different date order or peso formats — check a known amount and a date after the 12th.

FAQ

Fecha de operación or fecha valor?

Keep both if the PDF has them. Most bookkeeping uses the operation date unless your accountant asks for the value date.

Why are the amounts huge in Excel?

The file is a European CSV. Open it with a semicolon separator, or choose “Excel in Europe” in the tool.

Can I convert a credit-card extracto?

Yes if it is a text PDF, but the columns differ (no running account saldo in the same way). Convert it to a separate file.
