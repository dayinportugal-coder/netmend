The Monzo statement format

DatesPDF statements use DD/MM/YYYY. The official CSV “created” field is an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp such as 2019-01-05T14:35:41Z, which is not your local UK time late in the evening (a payment at 23:30 UK can fall on the next UTC day).
NumbersUK format in the PDF and CSV: dot decimal (12.34). Amounts in the official CSV are signed (money out negative) and often in major units (pounds), not pence — check a known payment.

Where to download the official file

Official path, from Monzo Help (“Getting a bank statement”):
  1. Tap Home in the app menu bar.
  2. Tap the three dots underneath your card.
  3. Tap Bank statements.
  4. Choose the date range and the format: PDF, CSV or QIF.

Pots have statements too. Monzo says statements are original legal documents (useful for a mortgage or visa) and that the name and address printed are those on the final day of the range you picked. Closed accounts are emailed a password-protected history (the password arrives by text).

What the PDF contains

A Monzo PDF typically lists Date, Description, Amount (GBP) and Balance (GBP), plus your details as they stood at the end of the period. It is the file to send as proof. Transaction descriptions are usually clean compared with older high-street PDFs.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The official CSV is a different, richer file than the PDF table. A commonly documented header is: id,created,amount,currency,local_amount,local_currency,category,emoji,description,address,notes,receipt,category_split

Use created (trim the time, and decide whether you want UTC or UK local time), description, amount and, if you spend abroad, the local_amount and local_currency columns. Category is Monzo’s own tag, useful for budgets, not a legal description.

What to watch for

  • Prefer the official CSV or QIF for bookkeeping, and the PDF for landlords, visas and mortgages.
  • Download Pots separately if you need to exclude or include them deliberately.
  • Our converter is for a PDF you already have (for example a period you can no longer regenerate).

FAQ

Does Monzo already give me a CSV?

Yes — PDF, CSV and QIF from Bank statements in the app. Convert a PDF only when you no longer have that export.

Why is a late-evening payment on the next day?

The CSV timestamp is UTC. UK time is UTC+0 or UTC+1. Convert to local time if you need the calendar day you actually spent.

What name appears on an old statement?

The name and address as they were on the last day of the range, not necessarily today’s details.
