Where to download the official file

Official path, from Starling Help (“How do I get my statements?”):
  1. Open your account menu in the top right of the Starling app.
  2. Tap Statements and documents, then Statement history.
  3. Select the month or months you want, tap Download statement, and choose PDF or Large print PDF.

Monthly statements cover full calendar months only. They are usually ready between the 4th and the 10th of the following month. If you select more than one month, Starling delivers them together in a ZIP file.

For a custom date range in the last 12 months, stay on Statement history and tap the Go to feed exports link. You can choose current month to date, last 3 full months, or a custom range, then PDF, large-print PDF or CSV.

What the PDF contains

From August 2026 Starling changed how statements appear in the app. Each official monthly PDF now carries a “True Copy” stamp at the top and a verifiable identification number, so a third party can confirm it with Starling. That PDF is the record: it is not the same file as a feed export. Transaction lines typically show the date, a description, the amount and a running balance, including fees such as overdraft or loan charges.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Starling still offers a CSV, but Help is explicit: a feed-export CSV is a transaction summary, not a verifiable statement. Use it for spreadsheets and accounting tools (Starling mentions Xero and FreeAgent on its statements feature page). Use the monthly True Copy PDF when a lender, landlord or accountant asks for an official statement.

Starling does not publish the CSV column layout on its Help pages. Open the first row of your own export and keep those headings. Do not assume it includes an opening and closing balance — that is why a conversion from the monthly PDF can still be useful: the PDF is the file you can tick against.

What to watch for

  • If you only need a spreadsheet of recent activity, generate the official CSV from feed exports and skip the PDF.
  • If you need a file that ties out, convert the monthly True Copy PDF and check every row against the printed opening and closing balance.
  • Several months download as a ZIP of PDFs, not one long statement — convert each month, then append the CSVs.
  • Pending card payments can appear in the app feed but not on a completed monthly statement. Do not mix the two without checking the dates.

FAQ

Does Starling already give me a CSV?

Yes, from Statement history → Go to feed exports, as PDF, large-print PDF or CSV, for dates in the last 12 months. Starling says that file is a transaction summary, not a verifiable monthly statement.

What is the True Copy stamp?

From August 2026, official monthly PDFs carry a True Copy stamp and a statement identification number. A third party can use that number to confirm the document with Starling. Feed exports do not replace that.

Can I get a mid-month official statement?

Full monthly statements are calendar months only. For a custom range, use a feed export (PDF or CSV). That export is not the verifiable monthly statement.
