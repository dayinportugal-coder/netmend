Where to download the official file

Official PDF, from Lloyds Bank (“Download statements”):
  1. App: log in, select the account, open the three-dot menu, then Download or print statement.
  2. Online banking: from the account page open Statement options and choose to print, download or order a paper copy (paper copies can take up to ten working days).

Lloyds says online banking keeps statements for up to seven years, and the app for at least ten. Credit-card PDFs may need the app or a request via chat — they are not always in the same online-banking menu as current accounts.

For a spreadsheet of transactions, use the desktop Export transactions option (CSV or QIF) on the account, for a date range the bank still offers.

What the PDF contains

A typical Lloyds current-account PDF lists Date, Description (and often a Type), Money In, Money Out and Balance. Only one of Money In or Money Out is filled on each row. Opening and closing balances sit in the statement header and should be used to check the file after conversion.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The official transaction CSV commonly has Date, Description, Type, Money In (£), Money Out (£) and Balance (£). Prefer that file. Use our converter for PDF statements, or for a period the export no longer covers.

What to watch for

  • Keep the PDF as the official document even when you work from the CSV.
  • Credit-card statements have a different layout (payment due, interest). Convert them to a separate sheet.

FAQ

Does Lloyds offer a CSV?

Yes, via Export transactions on the desktop site (CSV or QIF). Official monthly statements are PDFs.

How far back can I go?

Lloyds says up to seven years in online banking and at least ten in the app, for statements. Transaction exports usually cover a shorter window.

Money In / Money Out or one Amount?

The bank uses two columns. Our tool gives a signed Amount (money out negative) plus Balance.
