Where to download the official file

Official Halifax guidance, as reproduced in UK local-authority statement instructions that quote Halifax’s own “Download statements” steps (checked 13/11/2026; Halifax’s public how-to URL returned an error that day, so also trust the labels in your app):

In the Halifax app: sign in, select the account, open the three-dot account menu, choose Download or print statement, pick the statements, and follow the prompts. Paper copies are a separate “Order paper statements” option.

In Online Banking: sign in, select the account, open Statement options, then print or download a copy.

Menu names change from time to time. If you also see an option to export transactions from Online Banking on a computer, use it when it covers the dates you need. Halifax does not publish a public official guide to a CSV export, so treat the on-screen export dialog as the authority.

What the PDF contains

A Halifax current-account PDF is the official monthly document. In the Lloyds Banking Group family, the transaction table commonly prints Date, Description, Type, Money in, Money out and Balance, sometimes with (£) after a heading. Type codes such as DD, DEB or FPI often sit in their own column or at the start of the description. Confirm the headings on the first page of your own file — layouts change, and credit-card statements are a different document (Halifax tells card customers to open the full PDF, not just the summary).

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Halifax’s official statement is the PDF. There is no public official description of a Halifax CSV layout. If Online Banking offers an export, open the first row and keep those headings. After a PDF conversion, prefer columns that match the printed table (date, description, type if present, money in, money out, balance) so you can tick each row against the statement.

Do not add money in and money out into the same cell until you have checked the running balance. A blank in one of the two amount columns is normal.

What to watch for

  • Lenders usually want the official PDF, not a spreadsheet. Convert the PDF for bookkeeping; send the PDF when a third party asks for “your statements”.
  • After conversion, opening balance + money in − money out should equal the closing balance on that statement. If it does not, a wrapped description or a summary block has probably been read as a transaction.
  • Credit-card PDFs list payments, fees and interest as well as purchases. Those are not current-account rows — keep them in a separate file.

FAQ

Does Halifax give me a CSV?

The official statement is a PDF from the app or Online Banking. Some Online Banking screens also offer a transaction export; Halifax does not publish a public official CSV guide, so use that export only if you can see it, and keep the first row as your template.

Why is a date such as 5 Mar 26 showing as May?

The PDF is day-first. A spreadsheet in a month-first locale will swap any day below 13. Set the date column to DD/MM/YYYY or to text before you save the CSV.

Can I convert a photographed statement?

Not reliably with this tool. It reads the text layer of a digital PDF. A photo or a scan is an image, and needs to be typed or sent as a done-for-you job.
