Where to download the official file

Official path, from TSB’s “View and download statements” how-to, in the app:
  1. Tap the profile icon.
  2. Tap Inbox.
  3. Find and tap the statement.
  4. Tap the download icon (TSB notes this can look different depending on your device).

The same Digital Inbox exists in Internet Banking: after you sign in, open Your Inbox at the top right. TSB’s paperless pages say statements and correspondence are held there as PDFs that look the same as the paper versions, and that TSB will not alter or delete items in the Inbox. You can go paperless for some accounts and not others. A duplicate paper copy of a current or savings statement may be charged — see TSB’s charges guide if you order one.

TSB’s public how-to covers the PDF in the Inbox, not a spreadsheet export. If you see an Export control at the bottom of a transaction list in Internet Banking on a computer, that is a separate, on-screen tool: use it if it covers your dates, and treat its format list as the authority. We could not find a TSB Help article that documents a CSV layout.

What the PDF contains

A TSB statement PDF from the Digital Inbox is the official document: branding, account details, the period, and the transactions for that period. Descriptions can wrap onto a second line, and payment-type codes may sit beside the description. Credit-card or savings statements, if you have them, are separate Inbox items — do not merge them with a current-account conversion without labelling the source.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

There is no official public TSB CSV specification. After a PDF conversion, keep columns that you can tick against the printed statement: date, description, money in, money out and balance where those headings exist. Opening balance plus money in minus money out should match the closing balance on that statement.

If you used an on-screen transaction export instead, do not assume it includes the opening and closing balance printed on the official PDF.

What to watch for

  • For a mortgage or tenancy pack, download the Inbox PDFs. A spreadsheet is for you or your bookkeeper, not a substitute for the official statement.
  • Check the Inbox regularly if you are paperless — TSB emails you when something new arrives, but the record lives in the Inbox, not in that email.
  • Convert one statement at a time, then append the CSVs, so a missed page is obvious when the balances do not chain from one month to the next.

FAQ

Where are my TSB statements?

In the Digital Inbox: profile icon → Inbox in the app, or Your Inbox at the top right of Internet Banking. Download the PDF from there.

Does TSB give me a spreadsheet?

TSB’s public how-to only documents the PDF. If Internet Banking shows an Export control on the transaction list, you can use it, but TSB does not publish a CSV layout — check the first row of that file.

Are Inbox PDFs the same as paper statements?

TSB says paperless documents are in PDF format and look exactly the same as the paper versions.
