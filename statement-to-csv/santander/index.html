The Santander UK statement format

DatesUK PDFs are day-first. Some Santander UK PDF tables print the transaction date as an ordinal day and month without a year (for example “21st January”); the year sits in the statement-period header, so a January statement that also shows late December needs care. Official midata/transaction CSVs use a normal date column.
NumbersUK format: 1,234.56 (comma thousands, dot decimal). Choose UK/US numbers and a comma CSV, unless your Excel is set to a European locale.

Where to download the official file

Official midata CSV, from Santander UK (“Midata — helping you compare current accounts”):
  1. Log in to Online Banking and open the My Accounts and Transactions tab.
  2. Choose the current account, then Download transactions above the table.
  3. In Download to, select midata (CSV) and download.

Midata covers up to 12 months. Personal data such as your name, address and account number is masked, and some sensitive transactions are masked too — so midata is for comparison and analysis, not as a legal statement. For the official monthly PDF, use statements in Online or Mobile Banking.

What the PDF contains

A typical Santander UK current-account PDF lists Date, Description, Money in, Money out and Balance. Only one of Money in or Money out is filled on each row. If the date column omits the year, copy the year from the statement period and be careful on statements that span two calendar years.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Prefer midata or the ordinary “Download transactions” CSV when you need rows for Excel, and keep the PDF as the official document. Our converter is for PDFs you were sent or for periods midata no longer covers.

What to watch for

  • Santander Spain and Portugal use a different online banking path and a European number format (DD/MM/AAAA, 1.234,56, débito/crédito or importe and saldo). If your PDF is in Spanish or Portuguese, set this tool to European numbers and “Excel in Europe”.
  • Do not treat a midata file as proof of address: personal details are intentionally masked.

FAQ

Should I convert the PDF or download midata?

Download midata (or transaction CSV) for spreadsheet work. Convert the PDF when that export is missing, or when you need the official document turned into rows.

Why is there no year on the date?

Some UK PDFs print “21st January” and put the year only in the header. Add the year before you sort or import, especially around New Year.

Does this work for Santander in Spain or Portugal?

The tool will read a text PDF. Switch to European numbers. The download menus are not the same as Santander UK.
