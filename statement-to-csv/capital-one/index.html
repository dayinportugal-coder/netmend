Where to download the official file

Official path, from Capital One Help (“Online bank statements”):

On the website: sign in at capitalone.com, click the account, then click view statements.

In the Capital One Mobile app: sign in, tap the account, scroll to the bottom of the page and tap statements.

You need to be enrolled in paperless statements to view them online. Bank statements are typically generated online by the 6th of each month. Savings products are generated quarterly if there is no activity to prompt a monthly statement. Direct accounts are automatically combined into one statement. Capital One says you can view up to seven years of statements online for active accounts.

That help page documents the official PDF statement, not a spreadsheet export. If you see a download or activity-export control on the desktop site, use it when it covers your dates, and treat that dialog as the authority — Capital One does not publish a public official CSV layout for US checking and savings on this page.

What the PDF contains

The official Capital One statement is a PDF. It typically includes the account summary, the period, and the transactions that belong on that statement. Combined Direct-account statements put more than one product on the same document — do not treat every line as a single-account transaction without checking which product it belongs to. Credit-card statements, if you have a Capital One card, are a separate product with a different layout (payments reduce the card balance; do not import them into a checking account).

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Capital One does not publish a public official CSV specification for US bank statements on its online-statements help page. After a PDF conversion, keep columns you can tick against the printed statement: date, description, amount or debit/credit, and balance where it is printed. Check the opening and closing balances before you import the file anywhere.

If you used an on-screen activity export, it may be a live transaction list rather than the official monthly statement, and it may not include the statement’s running balance. Compare it with the PDF before you rely on it.

What to watch for

  • If you are not enrolled in paperless statements, do that first — Capital One’s help says you need it to view statements online.
  • A January statement may not appear until about the 6th. Do not assume the PDF is missing until after that date.
  • For a tax year, download each monthly PDF and convert them one by one so a missed page shows up when the balances do not chain.
