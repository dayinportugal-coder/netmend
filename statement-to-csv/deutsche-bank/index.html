The Deutsche Bank statement format

DatesDates are TT.MM.JJJJ (DD.MM.YYYY), for both Buchungstag (booking date) and Wertstellung / Valuta (value date). They can differ. Do not read 03.04.2026 as 4 March.
NumbersGerman format: 1.234,56 (dot thousands, comma decimal). Choose “Excel in Europe” so the CSV uses a semicolon and a comma decimal.
RegionGermany (Deutsche Bank). Other country sites differ.

Where to download the official file

In Deutsche Bank Online-Banking or the app, open the mailbox / Postfach or the Kontoauszüge section and download the monthly PDF. That is the official document (Kontoauszug). Some movement views also offer a CSV or Excel export of recent Umsatz — if you have it, use that for spreadsheet work and keep the PDF as the legal statement.

What the PDF contains

A typical German current-account Kontoauszug lists Buchungstag, Wertstellung/Valuta, Verwendungszweck (often several wrapped lines), Soll (debits), Haben (credits) and Saldo. Soll and Haben are separate columns, not a single signed number. The Verwendungszweck is where SEPA references and counterparty names sit; they must stay on the same row as the amount. Credit-card or depot statements are different documents.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

After conversion you want one row per booking, with both dates, the full Verwendungszweck, Soll, Haben (or a signed Amount) and Saldo. Our tool outputs Date, Description, Amount (Soll negative, Haben positive) and Balance. If a wrapped purpose line becomes a second transaction, try “Every text row” or send us the PDF.

What to watch for

  • Set European numbers and “Excel in Europe” before you convert.
  • Reconcile: opening Saldo + Haben − Soll = closing Saldo on the PDF.
  • This is the German Kontoauszug. Deutsche Bank in other countries uses other online banking and may use different date orders.

FAQ

Buchungstag or Valuta?

Keep both. German bookkeeping often books on Buchungstag unless you are asked for Valuta.

Why does Excel show 1.234 as one thousand?

The number is German: 1.234,56 means one thousand. Use a semicolon CSV and a comma decimal (“Excel in Europe”).

Does Deutsche Bank offer a native CSV?

Sometimes, from a Umsatz export in online banking. If you have it, use it. This tool is for the official Kontoauszug PDF.
