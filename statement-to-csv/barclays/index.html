Where to download the official file

Official PDF statements, from Barclays Help (“How can I view Online Statements?”):
  1. In the Barclays app: Help/More on the home page, then Statements.
  2. In Online Banking: under your name, open Online Statements, choose the account, and download the PDF. Statements from July 2013 onwards are in the archive if you registered in time; they also sit in My Barclays documents / Cloud It.

For a spreadsheet of recent transactions (not the official monthly statement): open the account’s transactions, set a date range, then use Export All and choose CSV. That file is the one to import into Excel or accounting software when the period is still available.

What the PDF contains

A typical Barclays UK current-account PDF lists Date, Description, Paid In, Paid Out and Balance. Only one of Paid In or Paid Out is filled on each row. Payment references can wrap onto a second line under the same date.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The official statement is a PDF. The transaction CSV typically has Date, Description, Paid In, Paid Out and Balance. Prefer that official CSV. Use our converter for older PDF statements, or for PDFs someone emailed you.

What to watch for

  • Do not confuse the official PDF (proof of the period, opening and closing balances) with the transaction export (rows for a date range you choose).
  • If a CSV date has no year, add it before you sort.

FAQ

Can I download a CSV from Barclays?

Yes, from the account’s transaction list (Export All), for a date range the bank still offers. Official monthly statements remain PDFs.

Paid In and Paid Out vs one Amount column?

The bank uses two columns. Our tool outputs a single signed Amount (money out negative) plus Balance. You can split it back in Excel if you prefer.

Does this cover Barclays in Africa or the US?

No. Those are different banks and layouts. Check a date after the 12th and a known amount.
