Where to download the official file

Official PDF, from HSBC UK (“How to get a bank statement online”):
  1. App: log on → select the account → View statements → pick the month.
  2. Online banking: quick links → Statements → choose the type, year and month and download. You can view, download, print or share a statement as a PDF.

HSBC UK says you can access up to six years of statements. Digital statements are PDF. Other formats are offered from the transaction list, not from the official statement:

  1. Choose the account → Search and filter → set the date range → Search.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the list → Download → choose the format (CSV or Excel) → Continue.

What the PDF contains

An HSBC UK current-account PDF is the official period document (opening and closing balances, the month’s transactions, any interest or charges). Transaction rows typically show a date, a description and an amount or paid in/out, with a running balance. Credit-card statements are a different document (payment due, interest summary) — convert them separately.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The transaction download is often a file with no header row and three columns: date, description, and a signed amount (money out negative). There may be no running balance. Because there is no header, the first transaction can be mistaken for column titles if a tool “detects headers”. Prefer this official download when the period is available; use our PDF converter for older official statements or for PDFs you were sent.

What to watch for

  • When you open an HSBC CSV, do not tick “My data has headers” until you have looked at row 1.
  • HSBC says a current or savings statement may not be issued in a quiet period (no debits, balance between £0 and £1 million). The annual statement is still issued.
  • Other HSBC countries (Hong Kong, US, Mexico, France…) have different online banking and date formats.

FAQ

Is the official statement a CSV?

No. Official statements are PDFs. CSV or Excel comes from the transaction list, for a date range you search.

Why did my first payment become a column title?

Many HSBC transaction CSVs have no header row. Import with “no headers” and name the columns yourself.

Can I share a statement as proof of address?

HSBC UK says a downloaded digital statement is valid for that; they do not issue stamped paper copies as a matter of course.
