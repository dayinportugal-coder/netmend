Where to download the official file

In CaixaBankNow (caixabank.es / the app):
  1. Go to Cuentas → Mis cuentas and select the account.
  2. Use Buscar movimientos (or the equivalent movements search) and set the period.
  3. Download the official extracto as a PDF. If the screen also offers an Excel or CSV export of the movement list, use that for spreadsheet work and keep the PDF as the legal document.

What the PDF contains

A typical CaixaBank current-account extracto lists Fecha, Fecha valor, Concepto or Movimiento, Importe and Saldo. Importe may be a single signed column or separate cargo/abono columns depending on the product and period. Long SEPA references wrap under the concepto. Credit-card and account extractos should not be mixed in one file.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Our tool outputs Date, Description, Amount (signed) and Balance. Set European numbers first. After conversion, check that the last Balance matches the closing saldo on the PDF.

What to watch for

  • Do not mix an account extracto and a card extracto in the same CSV.
  • If Excel smears an entire row into column A, the separator is wrong — choose “Excel in Europe”.

FAQ

Is there a native Excel export?

Some CaixaBankNow movement views offer a file export. If you have it, use it. This converter is for the official PDF extracto.

What number format should I pick?

European 1.234,56 and a semicolon CSV, unless you are opening the file in a US or UK Google Sheet that expects commas.

Can I convert several months at once?

Yes, one PDF at a time, then concatenate the CSVs after each period’s closing saldo has been checked.
