Where to download the official file

Two official downloads, and they are not the same file.

Official PDF statements are available in the Internet Bank and the Nationwide app after you sign in. Look for Statements or Documents / My Documents — Nationwide’s public pages confirm that statements can be viewed, downloaded and printed there, including (if you registered in time) historical statements after an account is closed. The exact menu label can change; trust the labels you see once you are signed in.

midata transaction download, from Nationwide’s official “About midata” page, is only on the Internet Bank (not the app), and only for current accounts:

  1. Log in to the Internet Bank on a Windows or Mac computer.
  2. Select the current account.
  3. Under Transactions, choose Download transactions.
  4. Choose Download midata.

The midata file covers up to 12 months of current-account transactions and excludes the 31 days before the download. Nationwide does this because recent transactions are sometimes used to confirm identity. If you have less than 12 months of history, you get what you have, minus those 31 days.

What the PDF contains

The PDF is the official statement for that account and period. It includes the society’s branding, the account details and the transactions that belong on that statement. A month with a zero balance and no transactions may not produce a statement at all. Do not treat a midata file as a substitute for this PDF if a lender or a tax enquiry asks for statements.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

midata is a government-backed current-account download aimed at comparison providers, not a statement export. Nationwide asks you to send the file in its original format and not to resave it as another type if you are using a comparison site. Nationwide does not publish the column layout on that help page — open the first row of your own file.

For bookkeeping, midata is still useful for the months it covers, with one gap you must not ignore: the most recent 31 days are missing by design. For those days, or for older statement PDFs, convert the official PDF and check the totals against the printed balance.

What to watch for

  • Need the last month for a mortgage pack? Use the official PDF. midata will not include the last 31 days.
  • Need a year of current-account transactions for a comparison site? Use midata, and do not edit the file first.
  • Closed accounts: you must register for online access to historical statements within 6 months of closure (accounts closed on or after 2 February 2018); Nationwide describes up to 5 years of statements in that process.

FAQ

Can I download a Nationwide statement as CSV?

You can download current-account transactions as midata from the Internet Bank (Transactions → Download transactions → Download midata). That is not the official statement, and it excludes the last 31 days. Statements themselves are PDFs.

Why are the last few weeks missing from my midata file?

Nationwide leaves out the 31 days before the download on purpose, because some firms use recent transactions to confirm identity. Download the latest PDF statement for those days.

Does midata include savings or credit cards?

Nationwide’s midata page says it is currently only for current accounts.
