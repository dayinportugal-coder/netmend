Where to download the official file

Two official files, from U.S. Bank’s knowledge base.

E-statements (PDF) — “How do I view, print and download electronic statements and other documents?”:

  1. In online banking, select Accounts from the top menu. In the U.S. Bank Mobile App, open the main menu and choose Statements & docs.
  2. Select the document type, then the account, then the year if you need older documents.
  3. Open the link to view, print or download the document.

You can view up to seven years of e-statements. The latest e-statement is available after the billing cycle closes. A certificate of deposit does not have regular statements (maturity notices and a year-end interest statement instead).

Transaction history download — “How do I download my transaction history?”. This is not available in the mobile app. In online banking you can download up to three years, as Spreadsheet (.CSV), Quicken (.QFX) or QuickBooks (.qbo):

  1. Select the account.
  2. In the Transactions tile, select the download icon in the upper-right corner.
  3. Choose the date range and file format, then Download.

For transactions more than three years old, U.S. Bank tells you to view your account statements (the PDFs). Direct Connect with bill pay for Quicken and QuickBooks is a separate enrolment.

What the PDF contains

The e-statement PDF is the official document for that cycle: summary, transactions, and the balances printed on that statement. It is the file to use when the three-year transaction download does not go back far enough, or when you need a document that shows the official closing balance.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

Prefer the official CSV (or QBO for QuickBooks, QFX for Quicken) when the dates fit inside three years. U.S. Bank does not publish the CSV column names on that help page — open the first row of your own download. The official file is a transaction list; it may not include the statement’s printed opening and closing balance in the same way the PDF does.

After a PDF conversion, keep date, description, amount or debit/credit, and the running balance, and tick both printed totals before you import the file.

What to watch for

  • If you need a spreadsheet for the last three years, use U.S. Bank’s own CSV or QBO download on a computer. Skip the PDF.
  • If you need year four to seven, or a statement someone already sent you as a PDF, convert the e-statement and check the balances.
  • Do not look for the transaction download in the mobile app — U.S. Bank says it is not there.

FAQ

Does U.S. Bank already give me a CSV?

Yes. In online banking (not the app), open the account, use the download icon in the Transactions tile, and choose Spreadsheet (.CSV). Up to three years. QFX and QBO are offered too.

How far back are official PDFs?

Up to seven years of e-statements in digital banking. The latest one appears after the billing cycle closes.

Why can’t I download transactions in the app?

U.S. Bank’s help says transaction downloads are not available in the U.S. Bank Mobile App. Use online banking on a computer, or convert the PDF statement.
