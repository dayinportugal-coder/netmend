FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no page limit, no watermark. We pay nothing to run it because it runs on your computer.

Which banks work?

Any statement that is a real text PDF (you can select the text with the mouse). We built it with Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, UK and US layouts in mind. Scans need OCR: we do those by hand in the paid service.

How is this different from other converters?

Most converters upload your statement to a server and charge per page after a small free allowance (for example 1 page per 24h without an account on bankstatementconverter.com, checked 12/11/2026). Here the file never leaves your browser and there is no limit.

Can I convert to Excel .xlsx?

The CSV opens directly in Excel. Pick "Excel in Europe" if your Excel uses commas for decimals.
