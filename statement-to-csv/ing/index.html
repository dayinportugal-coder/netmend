The ING statement format

DatesNetherlands official CSV: dates as YYYYMMDD with no separators (for example 20260131). If you open the file carelessly, Excel will treat that as a large number, not a date. German PDFs: TT.MM.JJJJ (DD.MM.YYYY) for Buchung and Valuta.
NumbersBoth markets use a European decimal comma. The Dutch official CSV is semicolon-separated. German PDFs print 1.234,56. Choose “Excel in Europe”.

Where to download the official file

ING Netherlands (Mijn ING) — official CSV (ING published a January 2026 format note):

  1. Log in to Mijn ING (ing.nl) or the app and open the account.
  2. Download or export movements and choose the CSV option.
  3. Keep the file as text until you have set the date column to YYYYMMDD → a real date.

Typical Dutch CSV columns: Datum;Naam / Omschrijving;Rekening;Tegenrekening;Code;Af Bij;Bedrag (EUR);Mutatiesoort;Mededelingen;Saldo na mutatie;Tag. Af Bij tells you whether the amount is Af (out) or Bij (in). Bedrag is usually unsigned, so the sign comes from Af/Bij, not from a minus in the number.

ING-DiBa Germany — in Online-Banking or the app, open Kontoauszüge / Postfach and download the monthly PDF. A transaction line typically has Buchung, Valuta, Auftraggeber/Empfänger, Buchungstext, Verwendungszweck, Betrag, Währung and Saldo.

What the PDF contains

A German Kontoauszug is a classic European PDF: two dates, a counterparty, a purpose line that often wraps, a signed or Soll/Haben amount, and a running Saldo. A Dutch official statement PDF, when you need one for proof, is separate from the Mijn ING CSV — use the CSV for Excel work.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

If you already have the Dutch official CSV, do not run it through a PDF converter. Import it with a semicolon delimiter, then split Datum with TEXT or DATE from the YYYYMMDD digits, and build a signed amount from Af/Bij and Bedrag. Use our PDF tool for German (or other) Kontoauszüge you only have as a PDF.

What to watch for

  • Never let Excel auto-open a Dutch Datum column. Force it to text, then build a date with =DATE(LEFT(A2,4),MID(A2,5,2),RIGHT(A2,2)).
  • ING Spain, Belgium, Italy or Australia use yet other banking sites. Check a date after the 12th and a known amount before you trust the first conversion.

FAQ

Which ING is this for?

The official YYYYMMDD CSV is ING Netherlands. The PDF section describes a German ING-DiBa Kontoauszug. Other ING countries differ — check your own header row.

Why did Excel turn my dates into 20,260,131?

Dutch Datum is YYYYMMDD with no dashes. Import as text, then build a date with DATE/LEFT/MID/RIGHT.

Is Bedrag already signed?

In the official Dutch CSV, usually not: look at the Af/Bij column and apply the sign yourself.
