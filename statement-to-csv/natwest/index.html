Where to download the official file

Official statements: in the NatWest app or Online Banking, open Statements (or View statements) for the account and download the monthly PDF. NatWest Help covers requesting a copy if a period is missing.

For a spreadsheet of recent transactions, from Online Banking:

  1. Go to Statements & transactions → View transactions.
  2. Choose the account and a date range.
  3. Export as CSV.

That export is not a substitute for the official monthly PDF when you need a legal statement.

What the PDF contains

A typical NatWest current-account PDF lists Date, Type, Description, Paid In, Paid Out and Balance. Only one of Paid In or Paid Out is filled on each row. References can wrap under the description.

Official CSV or Excel, when the bank offers one

The official transaction CSV commonly uses Date, Type, Description, Paid In, Paid Out and Balance, with DD/MM/YYYY dates. Prefer it for any period the bank still lets you export. Use our converter for older PDFs or for files a client emailed you.

What to watch for

  • If you also have an RBS or Ulster Bank statement, treat it as a sibling layout: check the header row rather than reusing a NatWest template blindly.
  • After conversion, check Paid In − Paid Out against the change in Balance, and against the PDF’s opening and closing figures.

FAQ

Can I get a CSV from NatWest?

Yes, from View transactions in Online Banking, for a date range the bank still offers. Official statements remain PDFs.

Is this the same as Royal Bank of Scotland?

Close, but not a promise. Check the header row of the RBS file before you reuse a NatWest mapping.

Why doesn’t the CSV cover four years?

Transaction exports are usually limited to a recent window. Older months have to come from the PDF archive — that is what this converter is for.
