Netmend Blur - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Netmend Blur is a browser extension published by Netmend (Pedro Carvalho). It blurs content on web pages you choose so you can share your screen, record or take screenshots without exposing sensitive information.

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data or web page content. Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (blur strength, which patterns to auto-blur, custom keywords).
  • For Pro users who turn on "Remember my blurs on this site": the site address (origin), CSS selectors of the elements you blurred and the position of blurred areas. The actual text behind the blur is never saved.
  • Your license key and the date it was last verified.

This data is kept in chrome.storage.local on your computer. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

The only network request

When you activate a Pro license (and about once a week afterwards), the extension sends your license key and the product ID to Gumroad's license API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to confirm the purchase. No page content, URLs or browsing history are sent. Gumroad's handling of purchase data is covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Permissions and why we need them

  • activeTab and scripting: to blur the page you are on, only after you click the extension or press its shortcut.
  • storage: to save your settings locally.
  • Optional site access (asked per site, Pro only): to re-apply your saved blurs automatically when you revisit a site you chose. You can revoke it at any time.
  • api.gumroad.com: license verification only.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no remote code, no third-party SDKs.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
