When doing it yourself is the right call

  • You have one or two digital PDFs, and you can already highlight the amounts as text.
  • The layout is a straightforward table: a bank statement, a card statement, or an invoice with a normal line-item grid.
  • You can spend 10–20 minutes checking that opening + movements = closing, or that each invoice gross matches the PDF.
  • The bank will still generate an official CSV or XLS for the same dates — in which case you should not convert anything; see official CSV vs PDF.

In those cases, paying is a waste. Use the official export, or use the converter below, save the CSV, tick the totals, and stop. Our price is not a better method for a file you can already tick.

When paying someone is the right call

  • Scans and photos. The free tool will not read them. Typing a packed folder of till receipts or photographed invoices is real work; that is the job.
  • Volume. A year of monthly statements, or a shoebox of mixed invoices, is several evenings of careful checking. A fixed pack of 20 pages from $20, 75 pages for $40, or 200 pages for $80 is often cheaper than your time, and the totals are checked against each document.
  • Layout that fights the tool. Multi-column statements, summary blocks that look like transactions, password files you would rather not wrestle with, or an invoice design with the tax box in a graphic.
  • A target template. If the delivery has to land in a QuickBooks or Xero import file, already mapped and already ticked, that is more than “get me some columns”.

What you should still expect when you pay: the official PDFs stay the official documents; the spreadsheet is a working file; and a human check against printed totals is the point of the fee. A cheap automated service that does not tick the balances is not a bargain. We delete the files after delivery; this page itself never uploads them.

A simple rule

If you can tick the file tonight, do it yourself. If you already know you will not, or you have tried the tool and the preview is wrong, use the paid option.
