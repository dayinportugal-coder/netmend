QuickBooks vs Xero when the source is a PDF

Neither QuickBooks Online nor Xero is “better at PDFs” in a general sense. They import different official files, they name the columns differently, and Xero documents a PDF bank-statement import that QuickBooks does not. This page compares those official paths so you can pick a method, not a mascot.

Bank and card statements

Both products prefer a live bank feed when the bank offers one. Use that for current months. The comparison below is for catch-up work, a PDF you were sent, or a period the feed does not cover.

  • QuickBooks Online does not import a bank-statement PDF as a PDF. Official help tells you to upload a CSV in a 3-column (Date, Description, Amount, with withdrawals negative) or 4-column (Date, Description, Credit, Debit) layout, or to use QBO / QFX / OFX from the bank. There are strict formatting notes — one date format, no weekday text, no zeros, Credit/Debit headers without the word “amount”, and card files must go into a credit-card account. See import a PDF statement into QuickBooks.
  • Xero documents a CSV bank-statement import (you map date and amount, usually with money in positive and money out negative) and, separately, an official article “Import a PDF bank statement”. If that PDF import is available for your organisation and it accepts the file, you can skip a conversion. If it is not, or it rejects the layout, convert the PDF to CSV and use the official CSV import. See import a PDF statement into Xero.

That PDF-import article is a real, documented difference in Xero’s favour — when it works. It is not a reason to switch products by itself, and it does not mean Xero will read every bank’s PDF. Always review the imported lines. Neither product will notice a dropped page for you.

Invoices and bills

On sales invoices and supplier bills, the two products are more alike than they are different: both want a CSV in their own template, not a PDF. You convert or type the line items, then import.

  • QuickBooks Online: Settings → Import data → Invoices (official required fields include invoice number, customer, invoice date, due date, item amount, item tax code; 100 invoices and 1,000 rows per import; no negative discounts). Bills are a separate import, with a separate sample file. Details in the QuickBooks invoice guide.
  • Xero: official CSV templates for customer invoices and for bills, with headings you should not rename. Repeat the invoice number on every line of a multi-line invoice, and use tax types and account codes that already exist in the organisation. Details in the Xero invoice guide.

Do not import an invoice as a bank withdrawal in either product if you also intend to raise the invoice or bill. That is how expenses get posted twice. The PDF conversion, in both worlds, is only the step that produces a working spreadsheet.

A fair way to choose a method (not a product)

If you already live in one of the two, stay there — this page is not a switch recommendation. If you are importing a bank PDF today: try Xero’s official PDF import when you are on Xero and it is offered; otherwise convert to the official CSV shape for the product you have. If you are importing invoices: convert or type the lines, then use that product’s sample file. If you are on neither product and only need a spreadsheet, use the tool below and keep a CSV plus a proof tab — see CSV vs Excel.

FAQ

Does Xero import PDFs and QuickBooks does not?

Xero documents a PDF import for some bank statements. QuickBooks Online’s official bank upload is a CSV or a QBO/QFX/OFX file. Neither product’s official invoice or bill import is “drop a PDF invoice here”.

Which official CSV is simpler?

QuickBooks is stricter about exactly two layouts. Xero lets you map columns on screen, which is more flexible and also easier to map wrongly (for example a running balance as an amount). Both need a check against the printed totals.

Should I pick a product based on this?

No. Pick a product on the rest of your books. This page only compares what each one officially accepts when the source happened to be a PDF.
