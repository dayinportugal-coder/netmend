What it does

Most “write my tweets” tools produce the same polished voice, with the same padded hooks. This one has a narrower job: learn your tone from posts you paste, then write 10 drafts around one idea, each built on a hook format we already use when we write for X.

1. Your voice

Paste a few posts you have already published. The model looks at sentence length, formality, emoji and how you start a line, then stays there.

2. Hook formats, not fluff

Question plus a numbered list, a concrete fact, numbered lessons from real work, a short twist, a tight first line. Structures, not canned topics.

3. Private by design

This page is static. Your posts, your idea and your API key stay in the browser and go only to Google’s Gemini API, with the key you supply. Netmend never sees them. There is no server.

FAQ

Is the Gemini API key really free?

Google offers a free Gemini API key through Google AI Studio. Limits and availability are Google’s, not ours, and they can change. This tool uses that key from your browser. We do not resell keys and we do not have a paid Google account sitting behind this page.

Where is my key stored? Do you see my posts?

The key is saved in localStorage in your browser. Your posts and your idea are sent only to Google’s Gemini API, from your browser, with that key. Netmend does not receive the key, the posts, or the drafts. There is no Netmend server for this tool. On a shared computer, use Remove key when you finish.

What counts as a generation?

One click of “Generate 10 drafts”. The free version allows 3 of those per day in this browser. The count is kept in localStorage, so it is per browser, not per person. Clearing the site’s data resets it, which we do not try to stop, but the fair use is 3 a day.

Will this post to X for me?

No. It writes drafts. You copy the ones you are happy with and post them yourself.

What does the $19 lifetime license include?

It is a founding price for the first 50 licenses, then the price is $29, paid once on Gumroad. The plan is to lift the daily cap and add extras such as saved voices and reply drafts. The free 3-a-day tool remains. You can pre-order now at portugaldiary.gumroad.com/l/leutz.

What hook formats do you use?

Structures we already work with when writing for X: a question plus a numbered list of concrete points, a hook that starts with a real fact or number, numbered lessons from work you actually did, an “anatomy of” list, a short opinion with a twist, and a very short first line that does most of the work. The model is told not to invent figures or testimonials.

Can I use it in another language?

This version of the tool is in English and asks the model for English drafts. If you need another language, email us; it is not in the free tool yet.

Hook formats, in plain language

These are the skeletons the drafts rotate through. They are structures, not topics to copy.

Question + listA real question on the first line, then 3–5 concrete points.
Concrete factOpen with a number or a rule that is in your material, then say why it matters.
Your setupYou (not a famous name we invent) plus a real figure plus the exact steps.
Lessons from workOnly if you did N things. “I did N. K things I learned.”
AnatomyThe N parts of a thing, named, not padded.
Short twistOne counterintuitive line, then the correction, then a rule you can use.
Very short hookThe first line does almost all the work.
