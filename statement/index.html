Formats this converter reads

There is no separate parser for each bank. A text PDF works when its rows match the patterns below. A logo, or a guide about a bank's own download menu, is not the same as support.

The file

A PDF whose text you can select. pdf.js reads that text in your browser. Image-only scans and photos are not read: there is no OCR. Optional AI mode still needs that text; it does not look at a picture. Password-protected PDFs fail until you open them and print to a new PDF.

Dates, copied as printed

Numeric: 12/11/2026, 12.11.2026, 12-11-2026, 2026-11-12, a two-digit year, or no year (12/11). The tool does not decide whether 03/04 is 3 April or March 4.

Or a day, an optional “de”, then a month in plain letters starting with jan, feb, fev, mar, apr, abr, may, mai, jun, jul, aug, ago, sep, set, oct, out, nov, dec or dez, then an optional year. 12 Jan 2026 and 12 de janeiro 2026 match. Accented names that do not start with those letters, such as março, février or März, do not.

Amounts

Two decimal places are required. European 1.234,56 when you choose that format, or automatically when the amount ends in a comma and two digits. UK/US 1,234.56 otherwise. A euro, dollar or pound sign may sit in front, or the word EUR, USD or GBP after the number. A minus, parentheses, or DR marks money out.

Column titles it looks for

The header cell has to be the title itself, not a sentence. If debit and credit are both there, or one of them plus a balance, amounts are placed by column. If those titles are missing: one number becomes Amount, two numbers become Amount and Balance, and three or more become Debit, Credit, and the last number as Balance.

FAQ

Does my bank statement leave my computer?

In the default mode, no. The page is processed locally: Mozilla's pdf.js reads the PDF in your browser and builds the spreadsheet on your computer. After the page and its libraries have loaded, that conversion still works if you disconnect. Nothing is uploaded to a Netmend server. Two optional steps do use the network: activating Pro checks the licence key with Gumroad, and Pro AI mode sends the extracted text to Google's Gemini API with your own key.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free converts one statement a day in this browser, and only the first page, to Excel or CSV. Statement Pro is $29, paid once. It reads every page, converts several PDFs at once (one sheet per file plus a combined sheet), and unlocks optional AI mode for messy text layouts.

Which PDFs and layouts does it read?

Text PDFs, where you can select the words. There is no separate parser per bank, and there is no OCR. A row is kept when it starts with a date the parser recognises and ends with amounts that have two decimal places. Dates can be numeric (12/11/2026, 12.11.2026, 12-11-2026, 2026-11-12, with or without a year) or a day plus a month name in plain letters. Amounts can be European 1.234,56 or UK/US 1,234.56, with optional euro, dollar or pound signs. Scans, photos and password-protected PDFs are not read. The exact header words are in the formats section above.

Does it check the totals against the printed statement?

No. The tool does not reconcile the running balance with the opening or closing total printed on the PDF. Compare them yourself before you bookkeep, and keep the original PDF.
