FAQ

Where do your leads come from?

Public sources only: company websites, open business directories and open data such as OpenStreetMap. Each row has the URL where we found it. We do not buy databases and we do not scrape LinkedIn.

Are the lead lists GDPR-friendly?

We only collect business contact details that the company publishes itself, and by default only generic addresses (info@, sales@). You are responsible for how you use the list in your country, including honouring opt-outs. We can include a "do not contact" check against a list you give us.

Do you use AI?

Yes, where it speeds things up: extraction, first drafts, translation drafts. Every delivery is checked against the source before it reaches you: totals for invoices, the live website for leads, a full read-through for translations.

Is the translation certified?

No. It is marketing and e-commerce translation, not certified or legal translation. Portuguese (European) is edited by a native speaker.

What do I need for a website?

Your text (or a few notes and we write it), logo, photos and contact details. You keep the files and the hosting account. A custom domain is optional and you buy it in your own name.

How do I pay?

Through Fiverr, Contra or Gumroad, so the platform protects both sides. We never ask for card details by email or on this site.

Can I get a refund?

If we miss the agreed scope and can't fix it in one round, you get your money back through the platform you paid on.

Do you do calls?

We work in writing (email or platform chat). It is faster for everyone and keeps a clear record.
