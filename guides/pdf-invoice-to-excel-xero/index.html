Three different Xero files

  • Sales invoices. Xero documents a CSV import for customer invoices in Central (“Import customer invoices”). Download the official template from Xero and keep the headings exactly as supplied. Repeat the invoice number on every line of a multi-line invoice. Dates and tax types have to match what your Xero organisation already uses. Treat the current template as the authority — Xero can change a column name without changing this page.
  • Bills (purchases). A separate import, usually from Purchases → Bills, with its own CSV template (commonly started from New bill → Import). Do not feed a sales-invoice template into the bills import, or the other way around.
  • Bank statements. A third file. Xero Central documents “Import a bank statement in CSV format”, and also has an article “Import a PDF bank statement”. For the CSV path, you map at least a date and an amount (money in positive, money out negative) and usually a description, then review the lines before you complete the import. Try Xero’s own PDF bank-statement import first when it is offered for your file; if it rejects the PDF or you need more control, convert the statement to CSV and import that. Bank lines are not bills. Importing a supplier invoice as a bank withdrawal, and then again as a bill, will double the expense.

A live bank feed remains the cleaner way to get ongoing payments into Xero. Use a file import when you are catching up, when the feed does not cover the period, or when you have a pile of PDF invoices and no bills on the system yet.

The converter below runs in your browser and never uploads the file. It is the same tool we use for bank-statement tables. It is useful when the invoice is a digital PDF with a clear line-item table. It is a poor fit for a photographed receipt or a designed invoice where the totals sit in a graphic. Use “Every text row” if a description wraps and the first preview looks short.

  1. Extract the PDF to CSV with the tool, or type the lines if the layout is too awkward.
  2. Copy the rows into Xero’s official invoice or bill template, one row per line item, with the invoice number repeated, a contact name that exists (or will be created) in Xero, and tax types and account codes your organisation already uses.
  3. Tick each invoice total against the original PDF. Then import in Xero and fix any rows the import report rejects — usually a missing contact, a tax type that does not exist, or a date format mixed between 03/04 and 04/03.

FAQ

Can Xero import a PDF invoice?

Xero has an official PDF import for some bank statements, documented in Central. Supplier invoices and customer invoices are imported as CSV from Xero’s own templates, not by dropping a PDF bill into the bank import.

What is the difference between a bill import and a bank import?

A bill is the supplier document you owe. A bank import is money that already left or entered the account. Raise or import the bill, then match the bank line to it. Do not import the same amount as both.

Why did Xero reject my dates?

Mixed day-first and month-first dates in the same column are the usual cause. Pick one format, say it on the import screen, and stick to it for every row.
